Many long-time residents of Southwest Virginia remember the era of dominance under Mr. McClure's leadership in the 1990s. Morgan-McClure Motorsports secured its very first win close to home, at none other than the Bristol Motor Speedway. Despite legendary driver Dale Earnhardt leading the pack for a majority of the Busch 500, Morgan-McClure racer Ernie Irvan pulled ahead for the win. Following this 1990 victory, Morgan-McClure Motorsports continued to win.
