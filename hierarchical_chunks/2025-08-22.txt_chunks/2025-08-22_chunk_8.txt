HONORING THE LIFE AND CAREER OF LARRY McCLURE Mr. GRIFFITH. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the life and work of Southwest Virginia racing icon Larry McClure, who passed away on June 25 at the age of 81. On March 21, 1944, Mr. McClure was born in the Harmon, Virginia, community, located in Buchanan County. After moving to Chilhowie in Smyth County during his childhood, Mr. McClure began to develop a passion for automobiles and racing. His years-long interest in cars eventually culminated in the co- founding of McClure Motor Sales. Along with his brother Ed, the McClures started the automobile business with great spirits and motivation. In 1980, Mr. McClure helped lead an effort with Tim Morgan to open Morgan McClure Chevrolet in Coeburn, Virginia.
