HONORING BARBARA KULIG'S RETIREMENT Mr. PAPPAS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the career of Barbara Kulig as she retires after twenty-six years of service with the Manchester School District. Barbara has dedicated her time as a Paraprofessional to making a difference in students' education and being involved within the community. As a lifelong Manchester resident, Barbara dedicated her career to public service and giving back to her community. She has spent twenty- five years teaching at Hillside Middle School, the same institution she attended for middle school, and she spent her final year teaching at Weston Elementary. She also previously served as a school bus driver in Bedford. She has left a lasting impact on the students she has worked with over the years and will be greatly missed by students and staff alike.
