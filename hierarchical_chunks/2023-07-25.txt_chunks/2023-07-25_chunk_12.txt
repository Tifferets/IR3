RECOGNIZING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE MARINE CORPS LEAGUE Mr. BERGMAN. Mr. Speaker, it is my honor to recognize the 100th Anniversary of the Marine Corps League. The Congressionally Chartered Veteran Service Organization over the past century has been critical in the care and welfare of our military, its Veterans, and their families. The Marine Corps League was established in 1923 by World War I hero, then Commandant of the Marine Corps Maj. Gen. John A. Lejeune. On August 4, 1937, it was Congressionally Chartered by the 75th Congress
