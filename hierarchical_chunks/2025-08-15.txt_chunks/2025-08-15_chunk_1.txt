Title: HONORING CABE LAMBERT; Congressional Record Vol. 171, No. 138 HONORING CABE LAMBERT Mr. EDWARDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Cabe Monroe Lambert, the last living World War II Veteran of the Eastern Band of the Cherokee Indians. Mr. Lambert was born on September 25, 1926, and currently lives in the Wolftown community on the Qualla Boundary in Cherokee, North Carolina. Mr. Lambert is deeply loved by his community and family.
