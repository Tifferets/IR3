HONORING SARATOGA COUNTY SHERIFF MICHAEL ZURLO Ms. STEFANIK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Saratoga County Sheriff Michael Zurlo, a lifelong resident of Saratoga County who has dedicated his career to keeping our communities safe, strong, and connected. Elected Sheriff on November 5, 2013, Sheriff Zurlo brought decades of hands-on experience to the office, having served as a deputy, sergeant, lieutenant, and senior criminal investigator within the Saratoga County Sheriffs Office.
