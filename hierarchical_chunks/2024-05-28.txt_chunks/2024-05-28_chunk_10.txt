MASTER OF CEREMONIES Congratulations Congresswoman Kaptur. COMMEMORATING THE CENTENNIAL OF THE U.S. BORDER PATROL Mr. THOMPSON of Mississippi. Mr. Speaker, with the introduction of H. Res. 1261, I join my colleagues on the House Committee on Homeland Security in commemorating the 100th anniversary of the creation of the United States Border Patrol. It is important that we honor the service and sacrifices of the men and women of the U.S. Border Patrol and their families, past and present. Since the U.S. Border Patrol's creation on May 28, 1924, Border Patrol agents have shown commitment and dedication to safeguarding our Nation by securing and protecting our borders. The U.S. Border Patrol has seen its mission expand greatly since its first stations opened in Detroit, Michigan, and El Paso, Texas, in 1924.
