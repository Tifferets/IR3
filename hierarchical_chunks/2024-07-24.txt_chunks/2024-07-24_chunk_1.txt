Title: CELEBRATING DAVID KRUG'S EXCEPTIONAL CAREER; Congressional Record Vol. 170, No. 120 CELEBRATING DAVID KRUG'S EXCEPTIONAL CAREER Mr. KENNEDY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate David Krug and his illustrious career at the City of Buffalo's Department of Permits and Inspection Services. After a career spanning almost four decades, Mr. Krug retires from his position as Coordinator of Plans. Mr. Krug began his journey four decades ago when he achieved the top score on the 1986 Building Inspector Examination. This outstanding achievement foreshadowed the next foul decades of an exceptional career
