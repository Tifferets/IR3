RECOGNIZING THE WASCO WOMAN'S CLUB Mr. VALADAO. Mr. Speaker pro tempore, I rise today to recognize the Wasco Woman's Club on their 100-year anniversary and to thank them for their significant contributions to the Wasco community. Founded in 1923, the Wasco Woman's Club was started by 13 young women who joined together to improve Wasco through volunteer service.
