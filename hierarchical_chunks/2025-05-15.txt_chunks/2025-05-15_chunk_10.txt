As a religious leader, advocate, mentor and friend, Monsignor Tucker has touched the lives of thousands. Through all of his good work he has instilled hope and promise in all of those who have had the good fortune to work with him. His spiritual guidance has nourished the souls of many and his compassion has encouraged others to give more of themselves. He has embodied some of the many mottos he has lived by including ``Success is in the effort, not the outcome'' and ``Joy, pass it along.'' Today, as he celebrates his 55th year as a priest, he continues to make a difference in the lives of others--his work a reflection of the heart of Catholic teachings. I am proud to extend my deepest thanks and appreciation as well as my heart-felt congratulations to Monsignor Robert Tucker as he celebrates this very special milestone. Happy 55th Anniversary and best wishes for many more years of success.
