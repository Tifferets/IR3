Over the course of his ministry, Monsignor Tucker did so much more than preside over Masses at the churches he led. He visited the sick at Charlotte Hungerford Hospital where he was chaplain, fortifying the spiritual centers of patients and their loved ones. He spent many summers working at Camp Workcoeman, one of the oldest continuously operated Scout camps in the country. And his weekly column in the Register Citizen, strengthened his deep connections to the community as well as parishioners and friends.
