HONORING IRIS BATHSHEBA RUEDA MARTINEZ FOR HISPANIC HERITAGE MONTH Mr. SOTO. Mr. Speaker Pro Tempore, Iris Bathsheba Rueda Martinez, a Mexican attorney, graduated from Universidad del Tepeyac, Mexico City, stands as a prominent figure within the Central Florida Hispanic community. Her illustrious career spans a remarkable 17-year tenure of service at the Consulates of Mexico in Orlando, Florida, and Fresno, California. Iris has dedicated her life to providing unwavering support and making a profound impact on the lives of her fellow Mexican compatriots living abroad while working closely with community organizations, legal experts, and government officials to address the unique needs of Mexican nationals living in the United States. Born in the vibrant heart of Mexico City, Iris has ardently maintained a deep connection to her Hispanic cultural heritage throughout her life journey. Her commitment to the promotion and celebration of Hispanic heritage resonates in every facet of her existence, with deep gratitude for the guiding influence of her faith in God. Throughout her distinguished consular career, Iris played a determinate and indispensable role in delivering humanitarian, community, and legal assistance to Mexican citizens residing in Florida and California.
