HONORING REBECCA KINNEY Ms. SPANBERGER. Mr. Speaker, I rise to commend the work of Ms. Rebecca Kinney, who has been an exemplary public servant over the last 37 years. Ms. Kinney has served as a printing specialist and program manager for the Publishing and Logistics Systems Management Section (ARDE) of Headquarters, U.S. Marine Corps (HQMC). Throughout her career, she printed more than 1 million documents for the U.S. Marine Corps. Ms. Kinney has demonstrated hard work, dedication, and a commitment to serving her country. Her efforts have been a great benefit to her team, the U.S. Marine Corps, and our National security. As she prepares for her next steps, please join me in thanking her for more than 37 years of contributions to the HQMC ARDE. Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating Ms. Rebecca Kinney on a well-deserved retirement.
