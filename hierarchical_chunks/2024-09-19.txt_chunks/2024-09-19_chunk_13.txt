RECOGNIZING THE REPUBLICAN NEWSPAPER'S 200TH ANNIVERSARY Mr. NEAL. Mr. Speaker, I rise to commemorate the 200th anniversary of The Republican newspaper in Springfield, Massachusetts. For 200 years, The Springfield Republican has been the standard for journalistic excellence in western Massachusetts and throughout the nation. Since its founding by the Bowles family in 1824, the paper has been in continuous circulation for two centuries--a feat that few publications can attest to.
