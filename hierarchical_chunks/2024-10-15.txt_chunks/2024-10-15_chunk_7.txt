Mr. Speaker, I am grateful for the opportunity to honor the exceptional accomplishments of Mr. Peter Jenks, who has recently been selected for the Top of Utah Military Affairs Committee's Western Big Hat Award. This award--first given to Admiral Chester Nimitz--recognizes individuals who have demonstrated outstanding service to their community. Anyone who knows Peter knows that there is no one more deserving of this award than he. I know Peter best for his dedicated service to my constituents in Northern Utah, but his influence extends far beyond this narrow snapshot of his career. Peter has served Utah's First Congressional District for three decades, working as district director for three consecutive Members of Congress. In this role, he has been critical to the success of this office and Congressional District, especially in serving the needs of our servicemen and women at Hill Air Force Base. Beyond work, Peter is consistently generous with his time. He is a current board member and former president of the Utah Defense Alliance where he leads efforts to engage state and local government and military and business leaders on defense and aerospace issues.
