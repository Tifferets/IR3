Peter has twice served as Honorary Commander at Hill Air Force Base, first with the 388th Fighter Wing and later with the 419th Fighter Wing and is universally loved by all who have worked with him. Those lucky enough to know him know there is no more loyal a friend nor selfless a mentor than Peter Jenks. It is my honor to recognize Peter's accomplishment and service for Utah's First Congressional District and our Nation's service members. Utah and the United States are better because of his work and example. I thank Peter for his service and sacrifice throughout the years. He is a model of courage, selfless service, and altruism from whom we can all learn.
