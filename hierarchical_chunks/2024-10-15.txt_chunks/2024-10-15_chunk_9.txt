HONORING ROBERT PEASE Mr. BARR. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor Robert ``Bobby'' Pease, from Flemingsburg, Kentucky. Mr. Pease is an exemplary teacher at Fleming County High School. Mr. Pease earned a Bachelor of Science degree in Agricultural Education and a Master of Arts degree in Career and Technical Education. He began his teaching career at Fleming County High School in 2003 and is a national board-certified teacher. He is a welding instructor at Fleming County High School and Director of Education at the Kentucky Welding Institute. In 2023 and again in 2024, Mr. Pease coached welding teams from Fleming County High School who were named the Kentucky FFA State Fair Welding Champions. Mr. Pease was appointed as a Curriculum Specialist by the Accrediting Council for Continuing Education and Training. He is an AWS Certified Welding Inspector and is certified with OSHA, an NCCER Master Trainer, and an NCCCO Practical Examiner. Mr. Pease was recently honored as one of 25 instructors nationwide to win the Harbor Freight Tools for Schools Prize for Teaching Excellence. This very prestigious and well-deserved award includes a $50,000 prize, to be shared with his school's skilled trades program. The award was created to recognize excellence among high school skilled trades teachers. More than 900 applications from all fifty states were submitted for the 2024 prize. In addition to all his professional honors. Mr. Pease serves as a mentor to his students and encourages them as he prepares them for good careers and a bright future. He is well-respected both at school and in the Flemingsburg community. I am honored to recognize him before the House of Representatives.
