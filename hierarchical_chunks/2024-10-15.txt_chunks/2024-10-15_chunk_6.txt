HONORING CC YIN'S 88TH BIRTHDAY Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, today I rise with my colleagues, Congressmen Ted Lieu and Mike Thompson to honor CC Yin as he celebrates his 88th birthday. Mr. Yin is a remarkable entrepreneur, community leader, and philanthropist whose impact is felt far and wide. Mr. Yin immigrated to the United States in the 1960s with only $100 in his pocket. He earned a mechanical engineering degree from the University of California, Berkeley, and later purchased a struggling McDonald's in Oakland, California. Together with his wife, he grew that single location into 30 successful franchises across Northern California. Their business achievements enabled them to focus on philanthropy and community leadership, particularly within the Asian American and Pacific Islander (AAPI) community. In 2001, Mr. Yin founded the Asian Pacific American Public Affairs (APAPA) organization, which promotes civic engagement, leadership, and education within the AAPI community. APAPA's mission has expanded nationwide, with multiple chapters across the United States. The organization provides scholarships and leadership training to empower the next generation of AAPI leaders. Mr. Yin's vision and dedication are an inspiration to our community and have earned him numerous awards and recognition from both state and national organizations. His leadership has made a lasting difference, and our communities are stronger because of his contributions. We thank him for being a beacon of light in our community.
