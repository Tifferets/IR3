RECOGNIZING JOAN MOORE Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the outstanding contributions that Joan Moore has made through her work with Gulfside Healthcare Services. Since February 2022, Joan Moore has touched the lives of over 10,000 hospice patients and their families through giving her time, talent, and treasure with Gulfside Hospice. With a servant's heart, Joan quietly, yet passionately finds ways to support one of the most vulnerable groups in her community--the elderly. Joan's journey into volunteering began later in life. At nearly 79 years old, instead of slowing down, she stepped up when she heard Gulfside needed volunteers. In just three years, she has donated more than 3,650 hours
