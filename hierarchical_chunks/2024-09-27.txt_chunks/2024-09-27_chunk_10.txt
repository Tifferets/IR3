HONORING RICHARD ``TURK'' BAIRAM FOR BEING NAMED HOOKSETT LIONS CLUB CITIZEN OF THE YEAR Mr. PAPPAS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today in honor of Hooksett Lions Club Citizen of the Year Richard ``Turk'' Bairam. For over 25 years, Turk has been dedicated to supporting Hooksett in any way he can. He continues to volunteer in his community to make Hooksett an amazing place to live and raise a family. A lifelong resident of Hooksett, Turk owned and operated his own auto repair shop for 36 years before retiring in 2006.
