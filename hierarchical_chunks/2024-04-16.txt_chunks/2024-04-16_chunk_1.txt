Mr. Speaker, April 19, 2024, corresponding to the 11th of Nissan, marks the birthday of Rabbi Menachem M. Schneerson, known as ``the Rebbe.'' It is celebrated worldwide and in all 50 states across our great country, including my home state of South Dakota.
