RECOGNIZING SOLEBURY TOWNSHIP POLICE CHIEF DOMINICK P. BELLIZZIE'S RETIREMENT Mr. FITZPATRICK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize an outstanding constituent of my district, Solebury Police Chief Dominick P. Bellizzie who is retiring on July 1, 2024 after 48 years of dedicated and faithful service to the citizens of Southeastern Pennsylvania. After high school, Dominick joined the United States Navy where he served prior to his law enforcement career. He found his lifelong career on May 17, 1975 when he started as a police officer at the City of Philadelphia Police Department, working in patrol, investigations, administration, and narcotics. Dominick has had extensive police training, including the Federal Bureau of Investigation National Academy in Quantico, Virginia.
