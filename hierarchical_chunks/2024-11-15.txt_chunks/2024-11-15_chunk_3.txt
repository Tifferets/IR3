CELEBRATING WARREN B. GELMAN'S INDUCTION INTO THE GREATER BUFFALO SPORTS HALL OF FAME Mr. KENNEDY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate Warren B. Gelman's induction into the Greater Buffalo Sports Hall of Fame--a fitting tribute to his lifetime of service, leadership, and dedication to the athletic community of Western New York. Mr. Gelman's life is a testament to the power of passion, hard work, and a commitment to others. As one of the founding members of the Greater Buffalo Sports Hall of Fame, Warren has been integral to the organization's growth and success since its inception in 1991. As chair of the Nominating Committee for the Hall of Fame, Warren helped shape its structure, bringing together a group of passionate volunteers who sought to honor the legion's greatest athletes and sports figures. His tireless work over the years has contributed to the Hall of Fame's enduring legacy. Mr. Gelman's contributions to Buffalo extend far beyond his work with the Hall of Fame. A former three-sport athlete, Warren excelled in soccer, hockey, and baseball before continuing his athletic journey at Yale University. There, he captained both the undefeated 1963-1964 freshman hockey team and the 1966-1967 varsity hockey team, which earned a ranking as the 5th best team in the East. Mr. Gelman is also a trailblazer for girls' sports in our community. In 1986, recognizing a void in opportunities for female athletes, he founded the Bison Girls Hockey Program, the first of its kind in Western New York. His efforts opened doors for countless young women to compete in hockey at the highest levels. Today, we celebrate Warren B. Gelman, and his induction to the Greater Buffalo Sports Hall of Fame. His contributions to Western New York's sports community, particularly his efforts to elevate the visibility and opportunities for women in athletics, have had a lasting and profound impact on the region. Mr. Gelman's legacy of service, leadership, and dedication will continue to inspire generations of athletes, coaches, and community leaders for years to come.
