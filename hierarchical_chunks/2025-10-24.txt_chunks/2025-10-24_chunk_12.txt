HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF SUPERVISOR JAMES M. ``JIM'' HOLLAND Ms. McCLELLAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life and legacy of the Honorable James M. ``Jim'' Holland, who faithfully served the people of Chesterfield County's Dale District for nearly two decades before his passing on October 14, 2025. I had the honor of working closely with Jim and witnessing firsthand his deep love for and unwavering dedication to his constituents. His kindness, humility, and steadfast commitment left a lasting impression on everyone who knew him, and his influence will be felt for generations to come. Born in Gates County, North Carolina, in 1951, Jim Holland served honorably in the U.S. Army's 18th Airborne Corps before earning degrees in accounting and business administration from Fayetteville State University and North Carolina Central University.
