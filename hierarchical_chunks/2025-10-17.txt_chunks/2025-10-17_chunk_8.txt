For 35 years, volunteers, churches, businesses, and civic organizations have come together under one mission: to build stability, dignity, and hope. Blue Water Habitat for Humanity stands as a testament to what can be achieved when a community serves alongside one another in common purpose. Mr. Speaker, on this milestone anniversary, I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring Blue Water Habitat for Humanity--its leadership, volunteers, donors, and partner families, for three and a half decades of faithful service. May their example continue to inspire service, personal responsibility, and the belief that stronger homes build stronger communities.
