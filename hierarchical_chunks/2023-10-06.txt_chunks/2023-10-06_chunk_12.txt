Barbara was chosen as an Assistant Mission Commander for the Capstone Military Leadership Program where she manages the development of over 50 Generals and Flag Officers. On one mission she was asked to speak about her experiences as a woman in the military with the women of the Zambian Air Force, a country where women were historically excluded from service. Barbara's 35 years of service to our country have led to a profound impact on her squadron. Travis AFB, and beyond. On behalf of the people of California's 8th Congressional District, it is my pleasure to honor and recognize Barbara Brewer for her positive contributions to our community.
