HONORING CHARLES BLOODWORTH HON. EARL L. ``BUDDY'' CARTER Mr. CARTER of Georgia. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor and remember the life of Charles ``Chuck'' Bloodworth, a former member of the U.S. Army and law enforcement. Mr. Bloodworth was known for his outstanding commitment to his military and law enforcement careers. He was a distinguished public servant who dedicated his life to serving the people of Georgia and the United States of America. He began his career in the U.S. Army and transitioned into different areas within law enforcement. Notably, Mr. Bloodworth worked in the Secret Service where he performed electronic crime investigations and worked to protect an array of individuals, including U.S. Presidents. Mr. Bloodworth's dignified service was recognized with Senate Resolution 111, signifying an exceptional life of public service. Senator Anavitarte's (An-Uh-Vi-Tart-Ey) tribute to Mr. Bloodworth highlighted his strong influence that was felt deeply throughout the communities that he touched. Mr. Bloodworth's legacy of great honor and patriotism will forever be cherished by the Georgia community and the United States of America.
