In addition, he has deployed in support of Operations Enduring Freedom, Iraqi Freedom, and New Dawn. As the Travis Air Force Base 60th Air Mobility Wing Commander, Brigadier General Salmi led the Air Force's largest air mobility organization in terms of personnel and was responsible for the well- being and readiness of more than 26,100 active-duty, reserve and civilian personnel, and their families. In addition, he managed $13 billion in capital assets, executed a $650 million budget, and oversaw the Air Force's largest medical center in the continental United States. Brigadier General Salmi's critical leadership and expertise not only made our country safer, but also helped improve the lives of the thousands of military and civilian personnel who work at Travis Air Force Base. Brigadier General Salmi's commitment to preparing the units at Travis Air Force Base to respond to any potential threat characterized his tenure as Commander of the 60th Air Mobility Wing.
