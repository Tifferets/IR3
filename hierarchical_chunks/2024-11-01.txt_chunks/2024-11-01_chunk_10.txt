Officer Santillano James has been recognized for her extensive accomplishments among which include holding office as vice-president and scholarship coordinator for the Latino Peace Officers Association-- Fresno Chapter, where she is committed to their mission of ``honoring family, education, and community through service and mentorship.'' Additionally, she was appointed vice-chair for Centro La Familia Advocacy Services and recognized as an Outstanding Board Member for her commitment. Today, we bid farewell and thank Officer Graciela for her service and going above and beyond for her community. Despite her career coming to an end, she continues to represent the Hispanic community as a bilingual and bicultural officer and aims to motivate individuals. As we honor the career of Officer Graciela Santillano James, I commend her for her advocacy and leadership. Mr Speaker, I invite my esteemed colleagues to join me in honoring the career of Officer Graciela Santillano James. Let us celebrate her diligence and devotion to the safety of others and educating her community.
