Mr. FONG. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the extraordinary life and legacy of Robert ``Bob'' Lechtreck, Sr., a decorated Navy veteran away recently, just months after celebrating his 100th birthday. His remarkable life reflects a century of service and deep devotion to both his country and his community. Born on April 2, 1925, Bob Lechtreck joined the U.S. Navy at the age of 17. He served in the Pacific Theater during World War II and saw combat in some of the most pivotal battles of the war, including the Battle of Okinawa.
