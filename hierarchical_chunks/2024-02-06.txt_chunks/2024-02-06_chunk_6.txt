Shockingly, the U.S. Veterans Administration in the early 2000's refused to grant ``presumptive eligibility'' for agent orange exposure health illness for veterans of AMERICA'S sea services. The VA's ``foot on the ground'' rule granted such coverage for Army and Marine Corps ground troops creating an outrageous disparity and discrimination against Navy and Coast Guard veterans of the Vietnam War. It took 17 years of constant advocacy to finally get Congress to pass a new law correcting this injustice. Paul's personal experience with the lasting impact of exposure served to enhance his advocacy ability, giving him a unique understanding of the hardship endured by many Vietnam era veterans. It was an honor for me as his congressman to help achieve this hard-fought legislative victory as an original cosponsor of this measure.
