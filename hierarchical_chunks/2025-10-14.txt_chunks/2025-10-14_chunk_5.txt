CONGRATULATING BILL ZORTMAN ON HIS 5000TH RADIO SHOW Mr. JOHNSON of South Dakota. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and congratulate Bill Zortman on the remarkable milestone of hosting his 5000th radio show. As a longtime voice behind ``It's Your Business,'' Bill has amplified leading voices to guide South Dakota listeners with practical wisdom and sound judgement. Bill's distinguished career in radio and television broadcasting spans more than forty years, including over fifteen years here in South Dakota. Beyond the microphone, he has demonstrated a deep commitment to public service and community leadership. After the terrorist attacks on September 11, 2001, Bill took a break from journalism and served as the Executive Director for the Red Cross
