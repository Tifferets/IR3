As the congregation concludes this year of celebration, it is fitting to honor its storied past and its promising future. For 300 years, Salem Heller's Evangelical Reformed Church has served as a bastion of faith and community for the people of Lancaster County. As generations continue to worship in this historic church, I know the congregation will continue to serve its mission to both the community and its faith. I extend my heartfelt congratulations to the congregation of Salem Heller's Evangelical Reformed Church and wish them continued blessings as they celebrate their 300th anniversary.
