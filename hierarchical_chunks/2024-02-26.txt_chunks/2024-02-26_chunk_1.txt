Title: COMMEMORATING THE LIFE OF NANCY FAYE CARLOW; Congressional Record Vol. 170, No. 34 COMMEMORATING THE LIFE OF NANCY FAYE CARLOW Mr. FALLON. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to commemorate the life of Mrs. Nancy Faye Carlow of Nash, Texas, who peacefully passed away on January 13, 2024. Mrs. Carlow was a loving wife, mother, and grandmother. Mrs. Carlow was born on April 27, 1944, in Maud, Texas, to H.J. ``Scooter'' and Hazel Smith. From a young age, she was known for being an academically gifted student. In 1962, Mrs. Carlow graduated from Maud High School as the valedictorian. She later received a bachelor's degree from Stephen F. Austin State University and a master's degree from East Texas State University. After graduation, Mrs. Carlow briefly taught home economics and worked as an elementary school teacher before becoming a bookkeeper for Dairyland Farms and Texarkana Tractor. Mrs. Carlow was a devoted woman of faith and was a lifetime member of the Maud United Methodist Church. While there, she taught Sunday school and led the Methodist Youth Fellowship church group for ten years. Mrs. Carlow was always a thoughtful woman who sought to put a smile on your face. Along with her husband James, she hosted daily happy hours for friends and loved ones so they could spend quality time together during the pandemic. She also started a weekly tradition of sending flowers to others so that they wouldn't feel alone. You could always count on her to help guide you throughout life and encourage you to do the right thing. Mrs. Carlow's tragic passing leaves behind a legacy of hard work, entrepreneurship, and family values that can never be replaced. I have requested the United States flag to be flown over our Nation's Capitol in recognition of Mrs. Carlow's beautiful and uplifting life. She will be dearly missed by her friends, family, and all who knew her.
