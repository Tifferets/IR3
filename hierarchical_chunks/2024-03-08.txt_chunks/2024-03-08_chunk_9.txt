HONORING ROBERT WILLARD Ms. TENNEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize an outstanding New Yorker, Attica Fire Department Member, Robert Willard, for his 50 years On Saturday, March 9, 2024, members past and present from the Attica Fire Department will gather to honor him for his service. Robert Willard was born in Batavia, New York and raised in the Town of Attica in Wyoming County, New York, and began his service as a volunteer in 1973 with the Attica Fire Department.
