As if her professional accomplishments were not enough, Karen completed 12 marathons including the Boston, New York and Marine Corps marathons and brought her love of running to Washington. In fact, she even ran with President Bill Clinton and described the President as ``a whole lot faster than he looks.'' In 1993, Karen became the congressional correspondent for The Sun until 1998, and its deputy Washington bureau chief from 1999 until 2002. From 2008 until retiring in 2020, she was a WYPR talk show host, reporter and assignment editor. She was even the subject of a PBS special, which was based on a very personal piece written by Karen. In these times of political turmoil, it was inspiring to read Karen's story and see what it means to live a life well lived. Fair winds and following seas to Karen, and may her memory be a blessing to her husband and United States Naval Academy adjunct professor, Alan Friedman, and her brother, Richard Argy.
