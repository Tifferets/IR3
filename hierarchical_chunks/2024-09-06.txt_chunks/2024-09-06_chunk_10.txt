] HONORING PAT SMITH Mr. SMITH of New Jersey. Mr. Speaker, I would like to take a moment to recognize the incredible life and the amazing contributions of my friend Pat Smith, who passed away peacefully on August 17th. Tomorrow, September 7th there will be a ``Celebration of Life'' to honor and pay tribute to the remarkable Pat Smith. Pat Smith's extraordinary work for patients, caregivers, and Lyme- literate doctors around the country has been transformational. An incredibly well-informed and tenacious leader, Pat fought effectively and relentlessly for greater diagnosis, treatments, and outcomes for Lyme patients. Because of Pat Smith, legislation to better meet the needs of Lyme patients is now the law of the land.
