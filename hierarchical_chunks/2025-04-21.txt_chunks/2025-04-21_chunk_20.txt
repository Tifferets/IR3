Anthony was one of many firefighters in the department who raised awareness about men's health issues during the month of November each year. His enthusiastic support for the campaign titled ``Movember'' is apparent in his most recent fire department photo, which now memorializes his notorious sense of humor. Anthony was known as a lion of a man with a big heart and warm personality. He will always be remembered as a kind and selfless individual who dedicated his life to serving others. Our communities will miss his presence deeply. He had a deep devotion to public service and his passing is an immeasurable loss for everyone around him, including his colleagues at the Fremont Fire Department. We send our deepest condolences to Anthony's wife, Erin, and his two daughters, Savannah and Abigail, as well as his other surviving family, friends, and fellow firefighters who were lucky to have Anthony in their lives.
