REMEMBERING FIREFIGHTER ANTHONY GANZLER Mr. SWALWELL. Mr. Speaker, I rise with my colleague Congressman Ro Khanna to recognize Fremont Firefighter Anthony Ganzler, on the occasion of his passing on March 17, 2025, at the age of 42. Anthony joined the Fremont Fire Department in 2019, serving on the Department's Special Operations Task Force. Prior to joining the Fremont Fire Department, Anthony worked at the El Dorado County Fire Protection District as a Firefighter/Emergency Medical Technician from 2015 to 2019. Predating his time as a firefighter, Anthony served in the United States Army's 173rd Airborne Division for more than eight years.
