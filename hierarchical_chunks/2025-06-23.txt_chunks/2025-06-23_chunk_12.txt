Her daughter recalls one specific tradition of setting up and reciting the Nobena Nino every year on the front porch. The Nativity they used was given to Ancin by her mom and is now with her grandniece Norene Reyes Aldridge. Her children and her grandchildren credit Ancin for their love of dancing and for the recipe for the best homemade flour titiyas served during large celebrations. Although she was the oldest, only she and her youngest sibling Maria Tanaka Diaz Nauta remain. She has survived all three of her sons yet remains loving and faithful. Her eyes and smile speak genuinely when she bids goodbye with a ``Thank you, God bless you.'' Ancin attributes her longevity to two things: physical activity, and interaction with others, especially with the youth. Two wonderful lessons I think we can all take away from such a life well-lived centered on family and intentionally finding gratitude in every day.
