RECOGNIZING EVERGREEN FIRE/RESCUE Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Evergreen Fire/ Rescue for receiving the Mission Lifeline Emergency Medical Service Gold Award from the American Heart Association for a third consecutive year. This prestigious award recognizes agencies with an outstanding ability to quickly recognize a heart attack or stroke and provide high- quality care. In these high-pressure cardiac situations, timely care from an EMS team can be the difference between life and death. Since 2014, Evergreen Fire/Rescue (EFR) has been honored with this award four times.
