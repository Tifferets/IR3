CELEBRATING BARBARA L. McQUADE'S SERVICE TO HER STATE AND HER NATION Mrs. DINGELL. Mr. Speaker Pro Tempore, I rise today to recognize Barbara L. McQuade, currently a professor of law at the University of Michigan Law School. Her service to her nation and the state of Michigan is worthy of commendation. Born and raised in Detroit, Michigan, Barbara earned her bachelor's degree from the University of Michigan in 1987 and her J.D. from the University of Michigan Law School in 1991. She began her career at the law firm of Butzel Long in Detroit before serving as a clerk for the U.S. District Court for the Eastern District of Michigan.
