HONORING VERNON ``LEON'' DAVIS HON. EARL L. ``BUDDY'' CARTER Mr. CARTER of Georgia. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to mourn the loss of Vernon ``Leon'' Davis. Leon Davis was a firefighter for over 30 years in Blackshear, Georgia. Mr. Davis worked his way up to Assistant Fire Chief of Blackshear while serving part time over his expansive 30-year career. Sadly, Davis passed in the line of duty while helping his Blackshear community during Hurricane Helene. This Hurricane has hit Georgia and the Southeast region of United States hard. Without the tremendous efforts and service of people like Mr. Davis, our communities would not be able to recover. Mr. Davis was known to be an exceptional man by his friends, family, and all the people he worked with throughout his career. He has deeply touched the Blackshear community and will be greatly missed by many. Our community is forever indebted to Mr. Davis's service, and I send my deepest condolences to his family and friends.
