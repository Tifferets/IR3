Title: CELEBRATING THE 100TH BIRTHDAY OF HOWARD DAVIDSON; Congressional Record Vol. 171, No. 59 CELEBRATING THE 100TH BIRTHDAY OF HOWARD DAVIDSON Mr. FONG. Mr. Speaker, I rise to wish a happy 100th birthday to Mr. Howard Lee Davidson of Visalia, California, a veteran of World War II and a hero of our community. Howard was born in Visalia, California on April 2, 1925. With the entry of the United States into World War II, he proudly enlisted in the U.S. Army as Private First-Class Howard Lee Davidson. Howard would soon brave the treacherous Atlantic Ocean aboard the RMS Queen Mary vessel to join the war in Europe. On June 12, 1944, six days after the Normandy landings on D-Day, Howard was assigned to the U.S. Third Army, commanded by legendary General George Patton. During this time, Howard served as an anti-aircraft gunner in which he was part of the offensive across France, Belgium, and all the way into Germany. While advancing through the Ardennes Forest centered around Belgium and Luxembourg, Howard found himself in the Battle of the Bulge.
