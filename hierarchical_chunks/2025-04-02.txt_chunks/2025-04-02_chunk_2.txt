When he returned to the United States after serving in the war, Howard met his wife Stella Thrasher and the two were soon married. The couple had three children--Kenny, Danny, and Vicky, who is Howard's loving caretaker. Howard's grandson, also named Danny, followed his grandfather's dedication to patriotism and currently serves as Staff Sergeant at Eielson Air Force Base in Alaska. In our country's proud history, it is so often the legacy of service among families which keeps our Nation safe and our Armed Forces strong. I hope Howard looks back with pride on his years of service, not only for the great sacrifices and honor, but also the enduring legacy it fosters for our Nation. Mr. Speaker, on behalf of a grateful Nation, I ask my colleagues here in Congress to join me in celebrating 100 years of Howard Lee Davidson's brave and patriotic life.
