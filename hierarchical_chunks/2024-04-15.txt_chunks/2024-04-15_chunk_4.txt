Mr. FALLON. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Reverend John I. Jenkins, C.S.C. of Notre Dame, Indiana and present him with the Congressional Patriot Award. Fr. Jenkins serves as the 17th President of the University of Notre Dame, and he has dedicated himself to faithfully serving and leading our community. He will step down as president in June 2024.
