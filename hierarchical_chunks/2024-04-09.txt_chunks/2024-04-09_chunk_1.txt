Title: CELEBRATING "ELIZABETH'S" AND "THE OLDE PINK HOUSE"; Congressional Record Vol. 170, No. 60 CELEBRATING ``ELIZABETH'S'' AND ``THE OLDE PINK HOUSE'' HON. EARL L. ``BUDDY'' CARTER Mr. CARTER of Georgia. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate two hardworking Georgia establishments that were named among the Nation's top 100 restaurants. Recently, the restaurant review website `Open Table' released its Top 100 Restaurants in America for 2023 list. Both `Elizabeth's' and `The Olde Pink House' made it on the list. The review states that `Elizabeths' has ``Southern hospitality, fine coastal cuisine, and well-chosen wines in a comfortable, elegant 1900 mansion have made Elizabeth on 37th a favorite destination in Savannah since 1981.'' As far as the `Olde Pink House' goes, the review states it is ``Savannah's only 18th-century mansion  is famous for memorable dining experiences featuring seafood caught daily in local waters, prime aged beef, and traditional southern fare.'' Congratulations to these establishments, and I wish them both continued success.
