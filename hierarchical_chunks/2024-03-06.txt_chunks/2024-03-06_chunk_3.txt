HONORING DENNIS JOHN BENIGNO HON. BILL PASCRELL, JR. Mr. PASCRELL. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to commemorate the inspiring life of Dennis John Benigno. A resident of the City of Clifton, Dennis John was debilitated by a traumatic brain injury when he was fifteen years old. The injury that befell Dennis John would have crushed weaker spirits. But instead of shrinking, Dennis John and his family used it to change the world. Back in 1984 when Dennis John suffered his injury, few people anywhere knew about Traumatic Brain Injury.
