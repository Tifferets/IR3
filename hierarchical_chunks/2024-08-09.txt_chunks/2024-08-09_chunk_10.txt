HONORING TRAVIS JORDAN PAMENI AND OWEN JAMES ELLIOTT HON. EARL L. ``BUDDY'' CARTER Mr. CARTER of Georgia. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to mourn the loss of two Georgia national guard soldiers, Travis Jordan Pameni and Owen James Elliott. Both at the young age of 23, Travis and Owen passed away in Baghdad on Wednesday from unrelated, non-combat causes. Mr. Pameni was assigned to the 1st Batallion of the 121st Infantry Regiment based in Lawrenceville, and Mr. Elliott was assigned to the 1st Batallion of the 188th Field Artillery Regiment based in Savannah. Mr. Elliott's unit was deployed in the Middle East in December of 2023 and focused on air defense artillery. We are extremely saddened by the loss of Specialists Pameni and Elliott.
