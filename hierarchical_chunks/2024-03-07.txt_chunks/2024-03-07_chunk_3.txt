There he focused his attention and expertise on issues such as agriculture, natural resources, immigration, and the environment. After 7 years at IACI, where he served as vice-president. Brent then created his own consulting firm named MPIdaho. As a consultant, Brent specialized in public policy development and strategic planning for private businesses in agriculture, taxation, and immigration. He also created the non-profit organization, Milk Producers of Idaho. More recently, Brent joined the University of Idaho's College of Agricultural and Life Sciences, as assistant dean for government and external relations. In this capacity, Brent worked extensively on a project called the Idaho Center for Agriculture, Food, and the Environment (CAFE).
