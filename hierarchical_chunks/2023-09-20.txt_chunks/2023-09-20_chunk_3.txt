HONORING THE 50TH ANNIVERSARY OF POLLACK INVESTMENTS Mr. BIGGS. Mr. Speaker, today I recognize the legacy of service and leadership of Michael Pollack, a resident of Arizona's 5th Congressional District, and I join him in celebrating his 50th year of business as the President and Founder of Pollack Investments. Pollack Investments specializes in commercial restoration and renovation projects, specifically long-term investments in shopping centers and other multi-tenant locations. In his industry, many developers will look at a property that has fallen into disrepair and only see a building ripe for demolition. But, in his own words, Michael sees ``each real estate project as an opportunity to enhance the fabric of Arizona's cities, one development at a time.'' That's not just a catchy line. After renewing these properties to their full potential, Michael insists that they are held for many years--even multiple decades--to ensure the long-term success of their surrounding neighborhoods.
