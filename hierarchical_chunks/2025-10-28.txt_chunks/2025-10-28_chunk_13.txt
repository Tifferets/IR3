From its collaboration with Arlington ISD on the annual Back to School Kick-off at AT&T Stadium, to its many ``Done in a Day'' projects, JLA consistently answers the call to serve families, women, and children in need. As the League begins a new chapter in its new home at Wellspring on Main--The Arlington Center for Community Engagement--it reaffirms its dedication to building stronger, more inclusive communities. Through leadership training, volunteerism, and a commitment to diversity, equity, and belonging, JLA continues to shape women into leaders who create lasting, positive change. Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in celebrating the Junior League of Arlington for its nearly 50 years of outstanding service, leadership, and community partnership. The League's unwavering dedication to empowering women and strengthening the Arlington community stands as a model of civic engagement and compassion for us all.
