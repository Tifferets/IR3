HONORING ROBERT ASHE Mr. THOMPSON of California. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Robert Ashe, whom I have named the California 4th District's 2025 Climate Crisis Champion of the Year for Sonoma County for his extensive volunteer efforts to restore and protect the Santa Rosa Creek and surrounding areas. He exemplifies the power of grassroots environmental stewardship as seen through founding the Friends of the Prince Memorial Greenway. Mr. Ashe was born and raised in Lancaster, California. He attended Antelope Valley College, where he studied police administration before working for over 35 years in the automotive parts industry in Los Angeles. After moving to Sonoma County, Mr. Ashe found a new calling as a custodian at Park Side Elementary School in Sebastopol, where he worked for 13 years.
