As the official spokesperson for the base, Mr. Woodward advised the commanding general on the effects of policies and actions on the base's relationship with internal and external audiences and was responsible for testing and executing crisis communication plans and procedures, serving as the base liaison with state and federal legislators, and overseeing a $1.3 million audiovisual contract. During his tenure, Mr. George Woodward led public relations efforts on critical developments at Sheppard Air Force base, such as the base's groundbreaking on a $27 million Child Development Center and the 82nd Training Wing's overhaul of the Air Force's Wartime Training Plan. Mr. Woodward also supported visits to Sheppard, including from the Secretary of the Air Force and Air Force Chief of Staff, Air Education and Training Command leadership, and foreign dignitaries. As the Director of Public Affairs, Mr. Woodward also led the communications efforts for the base's 75th anniversary and Guardians of Freedom Thunderbirds Air Show. On behalf of the entire Congress and a grateful Nation, as well as the thousands of Airmen who served at Sheppard Air Force Base throughout his eighteen-year tenure, I express my sincere gratitude and appreciation to Mr. George Woodward for his outstanding leadership and unwavering support for the missions of the U.S. Air Force.
