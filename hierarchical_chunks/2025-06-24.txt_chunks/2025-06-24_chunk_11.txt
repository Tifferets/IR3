Susan Newhart Elliott has served as a professor at the School of Law since 1994, is Director of the Zimmerman Law Library, and also serves as the University of Dayton Law Review faculty advisor. Susan earned her law degree from the University of Dayton in 1987, and was the law review's Managing Editor while she was a student. She has taught numerous classes in addition to serving as an Assistant Dean. Susan also holds a Bachelor's and a Master's degree from Miami University, as well as a Master's in Library and Information Science from Kent State University. After receiving her law degree, Susan clerked for Judge Walter Rice
