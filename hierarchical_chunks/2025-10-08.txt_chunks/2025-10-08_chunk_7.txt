Guided by her deep faith in Jesus Christ, Heil views her legal work as more than a profession--it is a calling. Prayer and biblical values shape her decisions and sustain her courage to stand for truth and justice on behalf of the vulnerable. Heil has practiced law for 33 years and is licensed in California, Tennessee, Virginia, and Missouri, as well as being licensed to practice before the U.S. Supreme Court. Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating this outstanding woman of distinction.
