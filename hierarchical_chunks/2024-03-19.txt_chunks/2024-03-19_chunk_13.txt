RECOGNIZING JULIE SHAMBARGER'S CONTRIBUTIONS RESTORING THE TIMES THEATER IN ROCHESTER, INDIANA Mr. YAKYM. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize Julie Shambarger for the incredible work she has done to revitalize downtown Rochester, Indiana, by spearheading the effort to refurbish and reopen The Times Theater in Rochester. As is the case with so many venues on Main Streets across America, The Times Theater was an iconic symbol of Main Street Rochester before closing its doors in 2014.
