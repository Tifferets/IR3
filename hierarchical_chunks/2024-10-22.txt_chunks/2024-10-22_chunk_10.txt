] RECOGNIZING DAVID BAKER'S CONTRIBUTIONS TO SCIENCE Ms. JAYAPAL. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate Dr. David Baker on winning the Nobel Prize in Chemistry. The Nobel Prize award recognizes Dr. Baker's tremendous achievement in utilizing computational protein design to turn amino acids into proteins. He has made significant strides in the field of biochemistry through his groundbreaking research and has trailblazed various methods to build new proteins. Dr. Baker is currently a Professor of Biochemistry at the University
