CELEBRATING OUR 2025 SPRING INTERN CLASS FOR THEIR SERVICE TO CALIFORNIA'S 20TH DISTRICT Mr. FONG. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the outstanding group of interns who have supported the work of the 20th District of California this spring. These bright and driven young people--serving in my Washington, D.C. office and my Bakersfield, California office-- have brought a spirit of dedication and enthusiasm that enriched our team. At a time when many young Americans are questioning the role of public service, their eagerness to engage with the federal government on behalf of their communities offers real encouragement for the future
