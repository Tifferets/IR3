HONORING BRIAN MURRAY FOR HIS DEDICATION TO SERVING OUR RURAL COMMUNITIES Ms. STEFANIK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Brian Murray for his dedication and commitment to serving our rural communities. Mr. Murray's dedication to serving our communities has been incredible through his work to help develop Upstate New York and the North Country. A lifelong New Yorker, Mr. Murray is from Peterboro, New York and currently resides in Lisbon, New York. He earned a Bachelor of Arts in financial economics from the State University of New York at Potsdam and went on to work as a commercial lending officer for a local banking institution for over 15 years. From 2002 to 2007, Murray worked as a USDA Rural Development loan specialist in the Canton Field Office before being named New York State Director for USDA Rural Development. During his time as State Director, Murray spent a year on the USDA RD Community Economic Development team in Washington, D.C. and has served as the Associate Enterprise Director. Mr. Murray's impact on our communities has been profound and has helped thousands achieve homeownership and brought in billions of dollars in investments towards rural interests. He's brought in this incredible investment through working with local leaders, state representatives, and federal agencies to achieve common goals of improving the quality of life for members of rural communities in New York. His contributions include supporting rural clinics and medical facilities across the state, providing first responders with essential equipment, vehicles, and facilities in an effort to keep communities safe, and supporting thousands of entrepreneurs in starting and expanding businesses.
