Amendment proposed: 12, page 28, line 36, leave out subsections (4) and (5) and insert—
“(4A) Regulations under subsection (1) may not amend, modify or repeal section 1 of the National Health Service Act 2006.”—(Dame Siobhain McDonagh.)
This amendment would prevent section 1 of the National Health Service Act 2006, which sets out the purposes of the NHS, from being amended by regulations.
Its effect would be to require changes to be made by an Act of Parliament instead.
Question put, That the amendment be made.
The House divided: Ayes 223, Noes 269.
Question accordingly negatived.
