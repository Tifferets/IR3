What recent steps he has taken to support the work of the British Council in response to the conflict in Ukraine.
We work closely with the British Council in Ukraine.
The British Council continues to provide online professional development to English teachers, reaching one in five English teachers in Ukraine.
The British Council’s teaching centre in Kyiv reopened online in April 2022, and it teaches English to approximately 500 students.
I thank the Minister for that response.
The British Council’s teaching and learning in difficult times programme has provided nearly 2,000 Ukrainian English teachers with help to support young people and children who have suffered trauma during the Russian invasion.
