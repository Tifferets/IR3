This is fuelled by social media and societal pressures on young people.
Such treatments often take place in unregulated high street environments and place pressure on the NHS.
When will the Government bring forward legislation to introduce a mandatory licensing regime, and will they allow for a debate in Government time on the Floor of this House?
The hon.
Member raises an important topic, which I know affects many of our constituents.
