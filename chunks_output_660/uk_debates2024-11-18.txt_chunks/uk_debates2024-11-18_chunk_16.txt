As an individual who signed up in just the past two weeks to be a reserve, this matter is close to my heart.
We are doing a review into reservists over the next couple of weeks, which will be linked to the strategic defence review, to find out how we can simplify the process and make it easier for people to join and serve the armed forces in a way that is befitting to them.
