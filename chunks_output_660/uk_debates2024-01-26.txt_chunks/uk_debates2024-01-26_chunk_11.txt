The combination of the terrible grief and, as my hon.
Friend  the Member for Congleton (Fiona Bruce)  said, loneliness in these situations—the shadow Minister,  the hon.
Member for Harrow West (Gareth Thomas) , called it “unimaginable”, which is an apt description—with the challenges of caring for a new baby must be incredibly hard.
My deepest sympathies go out to anyone who finds themselves in this terrible position.
The United Kingdom has a generous range of entitlements and protections designed to support parents to balance their family and work commitments and maintain their place in the labour market while raising their children—for example, maternity leave and pay, paternity leave and pay, and shared parental leave and pay, among others.
Maternity leave is rightly available  from the first day of a woman’s employment, recognising the special circumstances of pregnant women and new mothers.
Parental leave and shared parental leave are not day-one leave entitlements for mothers, fathers and partners; all parents must meet continuity of service requirements.
