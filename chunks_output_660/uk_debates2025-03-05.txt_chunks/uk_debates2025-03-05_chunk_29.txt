Harrow Crown court has eight court rooms and could help alleviate the capacity problems.
Sadly, it has been closed since 2023 as a result of finding reinforced autoclaved aerated concrete in the roof.
It was supposed to open last year.
That was then moved to April this year, and it is now being said that the court will not open for a further year.
Given the improved funding, could the Justice Secretary look at whether we can speed up the process of bringing that court back into action, and help the people who now have to go to Willesden, Amersham, Southwark and elsewhere in London for their cases to be heard, so that they can get justice at a local level?
