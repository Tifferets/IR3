I welcome the wider review of PIP and the consultation with disabled people, their organisations and clinicians, but given the wider review and that the Timms review is yet to report, why are we pushing ahead at this stage?
Because we have to begin to reform the welfare state, to help those who can work to do so, to protect those who cannot and to begin to put our  welfare state on a more sustainable footing.
The Bill protects existing claimants—they will not be affected by the changes.
It ensures that people have a right to try, and that those with severe, lifelong conditions never face reassessment.
It comes alongside the biggest-ever employment support for sick and disabled people.
Together, this is a fair and balanced package that meets the needs of existing claimants and reforms the welfare state for the future.
