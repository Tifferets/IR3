The courts will be involved and we will find ourselves in a very difficult, indeed intractable, situation.
As I have said before, setting this scheme in train without knowing what we would do when that happens is a bit like pulling the pin out of a grenade but not being prepared to throw it.
This is entirely foreseeable.
Let us find a way through this challenge.
