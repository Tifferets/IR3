What steps his Department is taking to protect public institutions and services from cyber threats.
The cyber threat facing the United Kingdom is intensifying.
State and non-state actors have targeted our critical national infrastructure, our businesses and even our democratic institutions.
The Government have introduced a new national cyber strategy, which takes a whole-of-society approach.
We have set out high standards of cyber-protection for our critical industries and, with the help of our world-leading agencies, we are offering advice to institutions, businesses and individuals on protecting themselves online.
Cyber-security is crucial not only to our defence sector but to others, including finance, energy and retail.
