I thank the Minister of State for her measured and careful answers to the urgent question.
It is important that her interest in this matter is put on the record.
Will she outline what discussions have taken place with our Israeli allies to renew peace talks, to allow both states to co-exist beside each other without the tit-for-tat action that has become normalised and yet is truly horrific and heartbreaking for all those who are losing loved ones in this conflict?
I thank the hon.
Gentleman for his question.
He is right that, when this violence occurs, the great tragedy is that civilians are caught up in it, especially where the Israeli Defence Forces are legitimately trying to defend themselves and, indeed, Palestinians from the terrorist threat.
The Foreign Secretary continues to have a strong focus on this and we are working with leaders and our allies around the world to try to find a solution.
