Gentleman is right to point out the importance of our defence industry and the need for us to support it.
Of course, the industrial strategy outlined eight growth-driving sectors that we believe can be turbocharged with Government support—defence was one of them.
We have published those sector plans, apart from the defence one, which will come shortly—he should look out for it.
We have significantly increased funding for defence, which will lead to thousands of jobs across the UK.
I will work closely with my colleagues in defence, particularly the Minister for Defence Procurement and Industry, to ensure that we procure UK jobs where we can and support our industry to grow.
